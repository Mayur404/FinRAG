The effective tax rate dropped from **14.8% in FY2021** to **-0.6% in FY2022**. This reversal is primarily attributed to the absence of the $700 million DOJ settlement payment in 2022 and the recognition of significant income tax refunds, resulting in a net tax benefit rather than an expense.
